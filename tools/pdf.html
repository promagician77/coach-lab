ASO Quick Reference

iOS App Store

  • Title (30 chars) — highest ranking weight
  • Subtitle (30 chars) — rotate seasonally
  • 100-char keyword field — comma-separated, no spaces
  • iOS 26: AI reads screenshot text for ranking
  • Seasonal rotation: football→basketball→baseball→camps

Google Play

  • Title (30 chars) — highest ranking weight
  • Long description (4K chars) — fully indexed like SEO
  • Store Listing Experiments — native A/B testing
  • Android Vitals (crash/ANR) suppress unstable apps
  • Review volume + velocity matters for ranking
