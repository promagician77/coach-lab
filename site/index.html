The Constraint

COPPA + Kids Category: If your app is in Apple's Kids Category or collects data from children under 13 (roster names, photos), Apple's guidelines (§1.3, §5.1.4) prohibit third-party ad SDKs and most analytics. This means standard paid-acquisition attribution breaks. Your marketing strategy must be built around organic channels from day one — which is exactly what you asked for, but the constraint is structural, not just a budget choice.

Positioning

Your competitors fall into two buckets:

  • Big platforms (TeamSnap, SportsEngine, GameChanger) — backed by DICK'S Sporting Goods or NBC, millions of users, feature-heavy, premium pricing
  • Free disruptors (Spond, Heja, TeamLinkt) — lean teams, word-of-mouth growth, free or ad-supported, strong community engagement

As a founder-led app competing on a small budget, you're in the second bucket. The playbook: own a niche, grow by community, and use AI to operate at 10× the content velocity of a 2-person team.

Screenshot Best Practices

  1. First 3 frames visible without scrolling — they decide the install
  2. Callout text at the top of each frame (iOS 26 AI now reads this text for ranking)
  3. Show the app solving a real problem: "One tap to check who's coming to practice"
  4. Rotate seasonally alongside subtitle changes
  5. Test with Store Listing Experiments on Google Play (no app update needed)

How the Approval Gate Works

1 Agent produces draft (blog post, metadata update, email)
2 Coordinator routes to approval queue (Slack channel or Notion board)
3 Founder reviews: approve, edit, or reject (target: ≤20 min/day)
4 Approved items flow to Distribution agent for scheduling
5 Analytics agent tracks performance, feeds back to Research agent
